Experience / About

A practice rooted in process, material, and wearability

I work across the full chain from fiber source to finished garment, combining technical method and creative direction to produce textiles and clothing with clear provenance.

Craft philosophy

I treat each stage as both technical and expressive work: preparation shapes possibility, spinning sets character, weaving defines structure, and sewing plus styling complete the story in lived form.

Values

Material Integrity

Prioritize traceable fibers and practical care over trend-driven output.

Process Clarity

Explain methods clearly so craft depth is visible and useful to others.
