Growing Flax for Fiber in the Chicago Region (presented at the 2026 HGA Fiber-A-Thon!)

Published 2026-03-28 · Tags: processing, weaving

The Handweaver's Guild of America hosts an annual broadcast to celebrate and fundraise for the fiber arts, featuring a diverse palette of craftspeople, artists, designers, creatives, and enthusiasts.

I was invited to present at their 2026 Fiber-A-Thon and share my experience cultivating, processing, spinning, and weaving with flax fiber grown in my front yard.
