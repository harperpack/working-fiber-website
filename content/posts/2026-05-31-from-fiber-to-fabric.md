# From Fiber to Fabric: Why Sequence Matters

Quality does not begin at the loom. It begins earlier, with preparation choices that determine what is possible in every stage that follows.

## Preparation controls consistency

When preparation is clear and consistent, spinning becomes stable and deliberate rather than corrective.

## Yarn decisions shape cloth behavior

Twist and ply influence drape, resilience, and how cloth behaves in construction.

## Garment outcomes reflect process integrity

Final style works best when each stage supports the next rather than competing with it.
