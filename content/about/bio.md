# About

I work across the full fiber chain, from growing and processing fiber through spinning, weaving, sewing, and styling finished garments. My approach combines practical craft rigor with an editorial eye for proportion, movement, and wearability.

## Craft philosophy

Each stage informs the next. Preparation decisions shape yarn behavior, yarn decisions shape cloth structure, and cloth structure shapes garment performance and style.

## Values

- Material integrity.
- Process clarity.
- Durable beauty.
- Practical use.
