Studio Notes: Testing Weave Density

Published 2026-06-15 · Tags: weaving, experiments, studio

Density testing changed how I sequence loom setup and finishing choices. Small shifts in spacing produced meaningful differences in drape and resilience.

Baseline observations

Lower-density structures moved more easily and finished with softer hand. Higher-density structures held shape better but required tighter consistency in yarn behavior.

Practical takeaway

Density decisions are strongest when chosen against intended use first, then tuned through short swatch cycles rather than committed in one pass.
